Your Privacy is Our Priority

At HireBridge, we understand the importance of your personal data and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

1. Data Collection

We collect personal information that you provide to us, such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, and professional details included in your CV or profile. This information is voluntary and used to facilitate your job search experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used primarily to:

  • Connect you with relevant job opportunities.
  • Send periodic notifications regarding status updates or new job listings.
  • Personalize your experience on the platform based on your preferences.
  • Improve our services and platform functionality through analytics.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies to enhance navigation, study site usage, and assist in our marketing efforts. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, but some features of the site may not function properly as a result.

5. User Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal data at any time through your profile settings. If you wish to close your account and delete all associated data, please contact our support team.
